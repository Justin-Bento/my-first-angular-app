Our story

Ember & Bean started in 2019 on a single 1kg roaster in a garage on Maple Street. We were tired of coffee that hid where it came from, so we set out to roast small batches and name every farm on the bag.

Seven years later, we still roast in small batches, most weeks no more than a few days before a bag ships. What's changed is the reach: we now work directly with growers across three continents, and the shop on Maple Street has become a place where the neighborhood starts its morning.
